Badger

An electric unmanned ground vehicle for small-unit logistics.

It carries 250 kg so a squad does not have to. Resupply, sensors, drone charging and light casualty evacuation, on one modular platform. Designed and built in Tallinn, Estonia.

Talk to sales See the specifications

TRL 6 and in field evaluation. Testing with Kaitseliit now, with Estonian Defence Forces trials in the first and second quarters. One of fourteen projects funded by the Estonian Ministry of Defence, chosen from thirty-one applications.

Four jobs a squad should not do on foot

Badger is a platform, not a single-mission vehicle. The bed and the power take whatever payload you fit to it.

Resupply

Moves ammunition, water and stores forward at up to 250 kg a run, and carries the load back out again.

Sensors

A mobile mount for ISR payloads. Put the sensor where the people should not be.

Drone support

A 15 kWh battery on wheels. Recharges and relays for UAS well forward of the nearest generator.

Light CASEVAC

Brings a casualty out of contact without putting a second crew into it.

Quiet, cool, and no fuel to bring forward

Low acoustic signatureAn electric drivetrain does not announce a resupply run. It works at distances a combustion vehicle cannot.

Low thermal signatureNo engine block and no exhaust. Far less for a thermal sensor to find.

No fuel chainCharge it from the grid, a generator or a vehicle. One less class of supply to push forward.

Power where it is neededThe pack is a field power source in its own right, for drones, radios and sensors.
