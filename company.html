Four people in Tallinn building a working vehicle

Ratel Robotics was founded in March 2023 to build one thing well: a small electric ground vehicle that takes weight off an infantry squad. The prototype drives. The next build goes to customer field testing.

Small on purposeA prime takes years to move from concept to a vehicle in a field. We do it in months, because there is nobody to ask for permission.

Built in EuropeDesigned, fabricated and assembled in Estonia, an EU, NATO and eurozone member state.

Dual-use from the startThe same platform carries a squad’s stores or an agricultural sensor rig. One vehicle, two markets, one development budget.

Grant-funded and pre-revenueBacked by the Estonian Ministry of Defence and EU regional development funding. Open to seed investment and to consortium partners.

Ratel and Mäger are the same animal

Ratel is the honey badger. Mäger is what Estonians call it. The animal is small, low to the ground, and carries on regardless of what is in front of it, which is roughly the design brief.

Estonian television called the vehicle Mäger when it first went on air. We use Badger abroad because it is the same word, and because it fits on a form.
