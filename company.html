Who we are

Ratel Robotics was registered in Tallinn on 16 March 2023. The company builds one product: a small electric ground vehicle that takes weight off an infantry squad. A full-size prototype drives today, and the next build goes to customer field testing.

StructureFour shareholders hold equal stakes. Decisions move directly from discussion to the workshop.

Built in EuropeDesigned, fabricated and assembled in Estonia, an EU, NATO and eurozone member state.

Dual-use designThe same platform carries a squad’s stores or an agricultural sensor rig, so both markets draw on one development programme.

Grant-funded and pre-revenueThe Estonian Ministry of Defence funded the 2025 development round, and an EAS regional development project (2021-2027.1.01.25-1260) runs to 31 July 2026. The company has no revenue yet and is open to seed investment and to consortium partners.

On the name

Ratel is the honey badger. Mäger is the Estonian name for the same animal.

Estonian public broadcaster ERR used Mäger when the vehicle first appeared on television in October 2025. The product is marketed internationally as Badger.
