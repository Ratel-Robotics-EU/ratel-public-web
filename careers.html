Engineering roles in Tallinn

Ratel Robotics is a four-person team in Tallinn with a driving prototype and a customer trial ahead. We are expanding the engineering team and accept open applications.

Send an open application

Disciplines

We do not publish narrow role specifications. Identify the discipline that matches your experience and describe what you have built.

Mechanical

Chassis, suspension, running gear and payload mounts, from design through fabrication and field testing.

Electrical and power

48 V traction, battery packs and BMS, charging, and power distribution for payloads.

Embedded and software

Motor control, teleoperation, communications, and the autonomy stack currently in development.

Systems and test

Integration, field trials, instrumentation, and resolving faults found during testing.

What to expect

Small and earlyThe company is pre-revenue and grant-funded, with four people. Work regularly falls outside a defined role.

On siteThe vehicle and the team are in Tallinn. These are workshop roles and not remote positions.

Short development cycleDesign, fabrication and field testing sit with the same four people.

Programme backingThe programme is funded by the Estonian Ministry of Defence and tested with Estonian units.

How to apply

State the discipline that matches your experience, describe what you have built, and explain your interest in this programme. A CV is useful; a link to your work is more so. We review every application and reply to each one.
