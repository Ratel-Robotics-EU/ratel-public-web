Where we need people

We do not write narrow job specs. Tell us which of these is yours, and what you have built.

Mechanical

Chassis, suspension, running gear and payload mounts. Design it, then go and weld it and drive it.

Electrical and power

48 V traction, battery packs and BMS, charging, and power distribution for payloads.

Embedded and software

Motor control, teleoperation, communications, and the autonomy stack we are building now.

Systems and test

Integration, field trials, instrumentation, and turning what breaks in a forest into a fix.

The honest version

Small and earlyPre-revenue, grant-funded, four people. You will do work outside your title because there is nobody else to do it.

Hardware, in personThe vehicle is in Tallinn and so are we. This is workshop work, not a remote role.

Short feedback loopWhat you design this month gets built and driven. Nothing waits two years for a committee.

It mattersEstonia has a reason to want this vehicle to exist. So does everyone who would otherwise carry the load.

One email, no forms

Tell us which discipline is yours, what you have built, and why this one. A CV helps, but a link to something you made helps more. We read every application and we answer. If the timing is wrong we will say so rather than leave you waiting.
